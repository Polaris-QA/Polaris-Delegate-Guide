    Pillar 4 of 6 · Resolution Writing

    One sentence. Every clause counts.

    A resolution is a single long sentence: a preamble that explains why, then numbered operative clauses that say what happens. This pillar takes you from first idea to final vote, and lets you build clauses that pass the dais's checks.

    Preambulatory: the why Operative: the what

    From idea to vote

    Six stages every resolution passes through. The rules at each stage differ by conference, so pick yours.

    Anatomy of a resolution

    An original sample written for this guide. Click any numbered part to see the rule behind it. Switch the format to see how HMUN and THIMUN lay out the same text.

    Phrase library

    Every opening phrase from the HMUN 2025 list and THIMUN Booklet 7, merged and tagged by source. Click a phrase to see how it's used, then send it to the clause builder.

    Clause builder

    Choose your committee and a phrase, then write the rest of the clause. The builder adds the correct punctuation and checks it against the rules the dais applies. Add clauses to assemble a full draft below.

    Preview & checks

    My draft resolution

    Preambulatory clauses always sit above operative clauses, and operative clauses are numbered automatically, so add them in any order. Saved in this browser only. HMUN bans pre-written documents: use this for practice, or for THIMUN drafts you bring to lobbying.

    From the final vote to the closing ceremony and the debrief back at school: how awards are decided, how to rate your own performance honestly, and how each conference becomes preparation for the next.

    Rate your conference See how awards work

    The endgame, step by step

    Six stages from the final vote to your next conference. Each one compares HMUN and THIMUN, and says plainly where our sources are silent.

    How awards are decided

    HMUN publishes its award criteria and exactly how many awards each committee gives. Choose a committee type to see the ladder, then open each criterion to see the evidence chairs look for.

    HMUN individual awards

    HMUN delegation awards

    Award-readiness scorecard

    Rate yourself on HMUN's seven award criteria after a conference or a practice session. The scorecard finds your weakest areas, points you to the right pillar, and builds a debrief you can share with your coach.

    Post-conference debrief

    Mira Costa's delegates write an evaluation after every conference: what happened in committee, how they'll improve, and how their teammates did. Fill these in and copy the full debrief, ratings included.

    Crisis note builder

    In HMUN crisis committees, delegates write secret crisis notes to advance a personal agenda. HMUN suggests a three-part letter and a one-line summary (“TL;DR”). Fill in the parts and watch the note take shape.

    The TL;DR is built from your requests. HMUN asks you to put it at the start or end.
